## 2. Control Objective

Determine whether periodic user access reviews were performed at the required frequency, completed by the applicable deadline, based on a complete access population, and supported by documented reviewer decisions and follow-up actions.

## 3. Control Criteria

The fictional Northstar internal control policy requires:

- Quarterly access reviews using a complete user-access population.
- Completion within 15 calendar days after quarter-end.
- Documented reviewer decisions and follow-up actions.
- Retention of supporting records for at least two years.

The applicable deadlines for the audit period are:

| Review Period | Quarter-End Date | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 2026 | March 31, 2026 | April 15, 2026 |
| Q2 2026 | June 30, 2026 | July 15, 2026 |
| Q3 2026 | September 30, 2026 | October 15, 2026 |

Q3 was not yet due at the September 30, 2026 audit cutoff. Missing Q3 review records at that cutoff must not automatically be classified as overdue.

The internal policy is the primary audit criterion for this simulation.

## 4. Audit Procedures

1. Identify the access review population for the audit period.
2. Validate population completeness, uniqueness, and the relationship between review records and the underlying user-access population.
3. Select six review records for judgmental testing, including records with timely completion, late completion, and potential evidence gaps.
4. Compare the recorded completion date with the applicable 15-calendar-day deadline.
5. Inspect evidence of reviewer identity, population coverage, documented decisions, and follow-up actions.
6. For reviews identifying access changes, inspect evidence of the associated remediation or removal ticket where available.
7. Distinguish an overdue review from a review that was not yet due.
8. Document exceptions, evidence limitations, and the effect on the overall control assessment.

## 10. Evidence Requirements

For each sampled review, retain or reference:

- Review ID, application ID, and review period.
- Applicable deadline and recorded completion date.
- Reviewer identity and sign-off.
- Underlying user-access population and evidence of its completeness.
- Number of accounts in the population and number reviewed.
- Documented reviewer decisions and business-need assessments where required.
- Identified access changes, remediation tickets, and evidence of closure.
- Test procedures performed, evidence references, results, and follow-up actions.

A summary status or source note should not substitute for the underlying evidence when the test objective requires proof of population coverage, reviewer decisions, or remediation.
